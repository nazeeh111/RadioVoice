# Source and additions

The retained implementation comes from [samyk/rflasermic](https://github.com/samyk/rflasermic/tree/7599f00a57b88cfcf8370cf88db6c04fa4e3da06), snapshot `7599f00a57b88cfcf8370cf88db6c04fa4e3da06`. The source is associated with RF-modulated optical audio experiments; the named authors are Samy Kamkar.

RadioVoice adds repository presentation, documentation and an offline preservation record, maintained by nazeeh111. These additions do not establish new authorship of the research method or independently reproduce the source-reported scientific results.

The generated Python flowgraphs retain their [GPL-3.0 terms](LICENSE-GPL-3.0) and SPDX notices. New documentation and presentation use [LICENSE-MIT](LICENSE-MIT). Other upstream assets and GNU Radio/device dependencies retain their separate terms; no broader asset grant is inferred. No radio/optical acquisition, transmission or live-device validation was performed for this packaging.
